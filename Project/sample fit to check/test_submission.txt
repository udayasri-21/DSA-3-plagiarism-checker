Plagiarism is the act of copying another person's work or ideas without giving proper credit. It is a common problem in schools, colleges and publishing where students submit assignments, reports and essays. Hashing converts a word or a phrase into a number so that searching becomes very fast. Copying from a friend or from the web without giving credit is a very serious offence. My own idea is that every class should keep a clean record of honest work and share it with the teacher each week.
